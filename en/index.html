Our solutions — P1

Sports Infrastructure
& Equipment

5TO9 develops and delivers multifunctional sports environments designed for schools, municipalities and public spaces. Through a combination of infrastructure design, equipment supply and on-site implementation, projects are transformed into durable, high-usage community assets.

The solutions include multi-sport surfaces, integrated activity zones and installation of sports equipment, adapted to different user groups and space capacities.

Each project is designed to create functional, accessible and long-lasting spaces that support everyday use and contribute to the development of local communities.

Our solutions — P2

Digital Solutions

5TO9 provides modern digital solutions designed to enhance communication, learning and collaboration across educational, institutional and business environments. By combining advanced equipment with installation and user support, each solution is delivered as a complete and ready-to-use system.

The offer includes interactive display technologies and adaptable setups that integrate seamlessly into different spaces, supporting efficient presentations, training and everyday operations.

Each solution is tailored to the specific needs of the user, ensuring reliability, ease of use and long-term functionality.

After installation our team trains teachers and staff in person, so the panels are used every day.

Our solutions — P3

Playgrounds &
Outdoor Spaces

5TO9 develops and delivers outdoor environments designed to support safe, functional and engaging community use. Through a combination of planning, equipment selection and implementation, each space is adapted to the specific needs of its users and surroundings.

The solutions focus on creating accessible and durable environments that encourage physical activity, social interaction and long-term use, contributing to the overall quality of public and shared spaces.

Our solutions — P4

Outdoor fitness
with real weights

5TO9 supplies and installs outdoor gyms by Finnish manufacturer StreetBarbell — machines with a patented weight system, tested to EN 16630, for parks, schools, campuses, hotels and residential complexes.

We combine strength machines, street workout structures, the inclusive Plus line for wheelchair users and the Kids line for the youngest — in a layout and colours that suit the space.
