FAQ

QUESTIONS
& ANSWERS.

Is the equipment certified?

Yes. The Standard, Light, Plus and Workout lines are tested to DIN EN 16630 for permanently installed outdoor fitness equipment (TÜV Thüringen, certificate no. 3510.02586.Z01). We provide the documentation for the selected machines with the quote.

How does the weight system work?

Each machine has weight plates adjusted right on it, so users choose the load like in a gym — from light to serious strength training. The system is patented.

What is the warranty?

5 years on the frame (powder or thermoplastic coating), or 10 years with hot-dip galvanising; 3 years on moving metal parts. The warranty applies with regular maintenance as per the manufacturer's instructions.

Can I choose the colour?

Yes. Machines come in standard RAL colours, or any RAL colour on request. Frame and moving parts are chosen separately.

Is there equipment for people with disabilities?

Yes. The Plus line (9 machines) has a fixed folding seat, so it can also be used by wheelchair users.

What kind of surface is needed?

Machines are installed on concrete floor plates or block foundations under the feet; the Light line can also stand without anchoring. We confirm the exact solution after a site visit.

Do you work with public procurement?

Yes. We work with schools, municipalities and institutions, including public procurement procedures, and prepare the technical documentation they require.

How do I get a price?

The price depends on the machines, quantity, finish and location. Send an enquiry and we will reply with a proposal and a quote.
