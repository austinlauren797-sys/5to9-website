FAQ

QUESTIONS
& ANSWERS.

Who distributes Buglo Play in Montenegro?

5to9 is the exclusive Buglo Play distributor for Montenegro — quotes, delivery, installation and support all go through us.

Is the equipment certified?

Yes. Every product is certified to the European EN 1176:2017 standard, with certificates issued by TÜV SÜD.

Can you equip a small space?

Yes. The compact lines (Mini, Single, Active) are made for small yards and preschools, and can also extend an existing playground.

Do you have equipment for children with disabilities?

Yes. The range includes inclusive equipment that lets children of different abilities play together.

Can the equipment be adapted to the site?

Yes. The manufacturer makes custom modifications when a site needs something that is not in the catalogue.

Do you work with public procurement?

Yes. We work with schools, municipalities and institutions, including public procurement procedures, and prepare the technical documentation they require.

How do I get a price?

The price depends on the equipment, the size of the playground and the location. Send an enquiry and we will reply with a proposal and a quote.
