FAQ

QUESTIONS
& ANSWERS.

How much space does a teqball table need?

The table itself is 3 × 1.5 m, and it needs a clear zone around it for players to move. We help you pick a suitable spot when choosing the location.

What is the difference between TEQ ONE and TEQ X?

Both are fixed models. TEQ ONE has a 6 mm HPL top and a 20 mm plexi net and is built for competition; TEQ X has a reinforced polyester top and a polyethylene net and is built for recreation.

Can the tables stay outdoors all year?

Yes. All models are made for indoor and outdoor use, waterproof and UV-proof, and rated from −20 °C to +40 °C.

Do you install the tables?

Yes. Delivery, set-up and anchoring are part of our service, together with support in using the table.

Do you work with public procurement?

Yes. We work with schools, municipalities and institutions, including public procurement procedures, and prepare the technical documentation they require.

How do I get a price?

The price depends on the model, quantity and location. Send an enquiry with the basics and we will reply with a quote.
